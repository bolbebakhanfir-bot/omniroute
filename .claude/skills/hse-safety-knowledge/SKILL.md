# HSE safety knowledge (water park, hotel, beach club — Qatar)

Read `references/knowledge-pack.md` and use the relevant section. It was compiled on
3 Oct 2026 from open public sources, and every fact links to its source in Section 12.

Rules when using it:
1. **Qatar law comes first, then brand standard, then international best practice. The
   stricter requirement wins.** For example, ISO 7243 WBGT limits are stricter than Qatar's
   32.1 °C cut-off for active roles.
2. Never quote a figure marked **UNVERIFIED** as fact. Say it needs confirmation, or leave it out.
3. Many figures came from search summaries. In formal reports, cite the primary source
   and tell the user to check the number against it.
4. Apply the corrections in Section 11 (immediate injury reporting under Art. 108, ISO 17842
   does not cover water slides, F770 not F1193 for operator training, double red = water
   closed, no green ILS flag).
5. If the user gives newer or local documents (MoPH pool regulation, Civil Defence code,
   brand SOPs in `.claude/hse-knowledge/`), they override this pack. Suggest updating the pack.
